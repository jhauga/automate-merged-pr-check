### Text that leaves this machine

The commit message, every file you change, and any issue or pull request text become public. In all of them:

- Write no local path, user name, account name, machine name, or email address. auto-check checks for these and refuses to commit, push, or open anything that has them.
- Do not narrate how the change was asked for: no "as requested", no quotes from these instructions, and nothing pasted from the failure report, which holds local paths.
- Use generic placeholder data in examples: Jane Doe, `jane.doe@example.com`, Acme Corp, `example.com`, `(555) 123-4567`, and paths such as `C:\Users\<user>\...` or `~/...` that match the platform shown.
- Say what was broken, what the change does, and why it is right, in plain sentences. Never use em dashes or en dashes.
